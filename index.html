Common questions

Is the price in Canadian or U.S. dollars?

Canadian dollars in Canada and U.S. dollars in the United States. The number is the same in both.

What counts as a user?

Anyone on your team who signs in: you, office staff, sales and service. You can add or remove people yourself.

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. Your site is live as soon as you sign up, written for your trade. You change what you want from a simple editor.

How does texting work?

It is an optional $10.00 a month. You get a business number and 250 texts a month. Texts you send and texts you receive both count. If you go past 250 in a month, your card is charged $10.00 for extra texts, which are used at 5 cents each. Whatever is left of that $10.00 carries over to later months.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your dashboard. Your account keeps working until the end of the month you have paid for. Nothing is deleted.
